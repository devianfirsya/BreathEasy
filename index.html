How It Works

Three simple steps.

BreathEasy makes breathing practice more interactive through a simple mechanical indicator.

STEP 01

Get Ready

Position the mouthpiece comfortably and prepare yourself for a controlled breathing exercise.

STEP 02

Breathe Through It

Follow the breathing exercise by inhaling or exhaling through the device as instructed.

STEP 03

Observe the Indicator

Watch the floating ball move inside the transparent indicator tube as you practice breathing.

Meet the Product

Thoughtfully designed for your breathing routine.

BreathEasy combines a compact ergonomic design with a mechanical airflow system and a visual breathing indicator.

  • Compact and ergonomic body
  • Detachable mouthpiece
  • Transparent visual indicator tube
  • Mechanical airflow components
  • Portable and easy to handle
Our Purpose

Making breathing practice more accessible.

BreathEasy aims to provide a simple and interactive way to practice controlled breathing. Through its mechanical design and visual feedback, the device encourages users to become more aware of their breathing patterns and develop a mindful routine.
